Rain Gutter Regatta — Wednesday, June 24th, 6:30 PM

Cub Scout Pack 26 in Dunellen is kicking off our summer program with a Rain Gutter Regatta at the Scout Hut on Pulaski Street. Scouts build their own little sailboats and race them down a water-filled rain gutter by blowing on the sails — it's a Cub Scout classic.

Please bring friends and siblings of all ages. No commitment, no cost — just come hang out, race some boats, and learn what Cub Scouts is all about.
